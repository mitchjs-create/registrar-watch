Marriage Ceremony Booking

What you will need to book the ceremony

A valid credit or debit card.

Please read the following before proceeding

  • We plan to marry in a licenced venue in the London Borough of Islington or City of London (the 'square mile').
  • We intend to be married more than 29 days from today.
  • We are both free to lawfully marry.
  • We understand that we both must give notice of our intention to marry with sufficient time before the ceremony.
  • We understand that if one or both of us are non-EEA nationals without the appropriate immigration status our notice period may be extended by the Home Office up to 71 days.
  • We understand that our booking is provisional and subject to review by Islington, who will contact us to confirm within two working days.
  • We understand that a non-refundable deposit is required in order to secure the ceremony.
  • If we are making a booking for a venue that is not Islington Town Hall, we understand we are booking the registrars' attendance only. We understand that we have to book the venue separately. You must ensure that the venue is available at your preferred date and time before you complete this form.
