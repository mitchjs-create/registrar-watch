Marriage Ceremony Booking

Introduction

  • Please be aware that you both must give notice of your intention to marry in sufficient time– at least 29 days before the ceremony. Book your notice of marriage
  • If you or your partner are not British or Irish citizens, or you do not have settled, pre-settled status or a pending application (made on or before 30 June 2021) under the European Union Settlement Scheme (EUSS) your notice period may be extended to 71 days by the Home Office.
  • If you plan to marry in a place of Worship and require a registrar to attend please contact us.
  • For ceremony bookings in venues excluding our three register offices, you do not need to have a celebrant for your ceremony. You will receive two (2) Registrars included in the cost of our ceremony. You will need to book this ceremony with the venue as well as with us.
  • If your legal paperwork has already been completed and you would like to enquire into booking a ceremony in the next 30 days please contact us
  • The London Borough of Wandsworth is committed to ensuring your information is protected. Your information will only be used to assist us with your Ceremony. We will not share this information with anyone else. Privacy Notice
