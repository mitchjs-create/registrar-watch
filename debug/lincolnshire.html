Ceremony Booking

Before you begin:

You can use our online service to book a marriage or civil partnership ceremony directly with us.

  • We recommend that you review the information on our website about Marriage and civil partnerships.
  • We also recommend you have read about the legal requirement to give notice -
    Understand Giving Notice: Get details on the process, required documents, and timelines.

When booking your ceremony the date and time you select will be reserved.  If you do not complete your booking the date and time will be released after 30 minutes.

How to Book:

  • Check Availability and plan in advance:
    Our online portal shows live availability. If your preferred date or time is unavailable, please select an alternative.
  • The time you select is the time your ceremony will start on your chosen date.
  • We recommend that you confirm and agree your ceremony plans with friends and family before booking. Changes to the date, time, or location will incur additional fees after a 7-day cooling-off period.
  • If you are booking a licensed venue, ensure the date and time have been confirmed with the venue before booking your registrars. This booking does not include the hire of external venues.
  • Ceremony bookings at Register Offices automatically include the room hire and registrar services.

Can’t find what you need?

Please note that due to increased demand, You Plus 2 ceremonies are fully booked until December 2026 onwards.

Please use the online booking system to check availability and book a ceremony.

For earlier ceremonies, you may wish to consider the Newport Room, Lincoln.
  • For ceremony dates within the next three months, please submit a booking enquiry form. These cannot be booked online.
  • For Exceptional circumstances ceremonies, you’ll need to contact us as these cannot be booked online.

Submit a booking enquiry form, and we’ll contact you to discuss your options.
